Dear Madumitha,

Happy Birthday, my dearest friend. I have tried so many times to put into words what you mean to me, and every time the words feel too small. So I made you a little book instead, a place where our memories could sit together, just like we do.

Thank you for being the person who sees me on my best days and stays on my worst ones. Thank you for the laughter that makes my stomach hurt, for the silly inside jokes nobody else would understand, and for the quiet moments when we didn't need to say anything at all.

You have this beautiful way of making ordinary days feel special. A walk, a snack, a random chat, it all turns into a memory when you are there. Your kindness is gentle but so strong, and I hope you know how many hearts you have touched, mine most of all.

I am so proud of the person you are becoming, and I know you will be an amazing physiotherapist, someone who heals with skill and with a caring heart. The world is lucky to have you, and so am I.

Whatever this new year brings, I'll be right here, cheering the loudest. I hope it is full of joy, good health, and every dream you have been holding close.

With all my love,
your best friend ♡
